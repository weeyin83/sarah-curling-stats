About the goal.

The goal counts active Scottish rinks, with The PEAK and National Curling Academy treated as separate rinks.

Based on Scottish Curling's official rink directory, cross-checked with Try Curling and venue information. Active means operating as a curling venue, including seasonal ice; check with the venue before travelling.

Locations come from public map records, including OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Each card links its coordinate source. Gogar Park's historical location is approximate.
